Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Sakeenah Uploader ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Sakeenah YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channel.

Data the App accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and read basic channel information (the channel name) for the owner's own channel. Scopes used: youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are used solely to publish the owner's own videos. The App does not collect, sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties, and does not access any other user's data.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
