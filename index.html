About

Sakeenah is a YouTube channel publishing the complete Quran recitation by renowned reciters with Uthmani script and translation, plus daily short clips of Quran verses and authentic hadith.

Sakeenah Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users.
